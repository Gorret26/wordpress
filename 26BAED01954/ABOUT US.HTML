ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning Skills. Building Futures.

Mulagi Vocational Training School brings education and practical skills together, helping learners grow in confidence and become self-reliant members of their communities.

WHO WE ARE

Mulagi Vocational Training School is committed to providing quality education and vocational training to learners. We believe that education and practical skills are important tools for improving lives, promoting independence and creating opportunities for a better future.

Through discipline, knowledge, teamwork and practical learning, we encourage learners to discover their talents, develop their abilities and become responsible members of society.

ABOUT MULAGI VOCATIONAL TRAINING SCHOOL

OUR MISSION

To equip learners with meaningful education, practical skills, and the confidence to build better futures for themselves and their communities.

OUR VISION

A future where every learner can use knowledge, skill, and strong character to contribute positively to society and achieve personal growth.

OUR VALUES

Discipline, knowledge, teamwork, excellence, respect, responsibility and self-reliance guide the way we learn, teach and work together.

SCHOOL HISTORY

Founded in 1985 by the Eucharistic Handmaids Sisters, Mulagi Vocational Training School was established when there was no other vocational school to serve the community. The school continues to equip learners with practical skills, knowledge and opportunities for a self-reliant future.

SCHOOL MANAGEMENT

The management team coordinates school activities and works with staff, families and the community to support learners, promote discipline and ensure the smooth running of the school.

SCHOOL LEADERSHIP

School leaders set direction, guide staff, support teaching and learning, and foster a positive environment where every learner can thrive and achieve her potential.
