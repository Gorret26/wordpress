LEARN BY DOING

Skills for Today. Confidence for Life.

At Mulagi, learners build practical skills, grow in confidence, and prepare for a more self-reliant future. We combine knowledge with practical learning to help learners develop skills that can be used in everyday life and the workplace.

OUR COURSES AND SKILLS

Our vocational programmes help learners develop useful skills, creativity, confidence and knowledge. Through practical training and guidance, learners prepare for employment, entrepreneurship and independent living.

HOTEL MANAGEMENT

Learners develop practical skills in hospitality, customer care, food service, housekeeping and hotel operations to prepare for careers in the hospitality industry.

TAILORING & GARMENT MAKING

Learners explore garment construction, fabric selection, measurements, sewing, fashion design and professional tailoring techniques.

SECRETARIAL STUDIES

Learners build communication, office administration, document preparation, record keeping, typing and office-support skills.

BAKING

Learners acquire practical baking skills, including cake making, bread preparation, pastries, food handling and presentation. They also develop creativity and business skills.

COMPUTER SKILLS

Learners gain knowledge in computer operation, typing, word processing, internet use, presentations and basic digital skills for education and work.

LEARN BY DOING

Our learning approach combines classroom knowledge with hands-on practice, guidance, teamwork and practical activities that prepare learners for real-life situations.

THE LEARNING EXPERIENCE

A Path from Learning to Independence

Every learner grows at her own pace. Our school values practical ability, supportive relationships and the confidence to keep moving forward.

01

BUILD UNDERSTANDING

Learners begin by gaining knowledge, understanding important concepts and receiving guidance from instructors to help them develop a strong foundation.

02

LEARN THROUGH PRACTICE

Learners put their knowledge into action through practical activities, demonstrations, exercises and projects that help them improve their abilities.

03

PREPARE FOR WHAT'S NEXT

Learners develop teamwork, discipline, confidence and practical abilities that can help them pursue employment, business opportunities and further learning.
